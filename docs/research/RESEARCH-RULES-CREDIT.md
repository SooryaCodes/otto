# Rules and credit research: job work, TDS, credit, consent, labour

Researched 15 Nov 2026. Each fact is followed by its source. **[sec]** = secondary source (trade press, law firm, aggregator). **(CA)** = confirm with our CA before it goes on a slide.

## 1. GST on job work (jeweller sends gold to karigar)

- **Section 143(1):** a registered principal may send inputs or capital goods to a job worker without paying tax. Inputs must come back within **1 year**, capital goods within **3 years** (moulds, dies, jigs, fixtures, tools excluded). The Commissioner may extend by up to 1 and 2 years. https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/CGST-Act-Updated-30092020.pdf
- **143(2):** "responsibility for keeping proper accounts" for those goods "shall lie with the principal". Same source.
- **Deemed supply, 143(3) and (4):** goods not back in time are deemed supplied by the principal to the job worker **on the day they were sent out**, so tax and interest run from that date. For gold, 3% GST on the metal (CA). Same source.
- **143(5):** scrap (gold dust, lemel) may be supplied by the job worker if registered, else by the principal. **2(68):** "job work" means a process on goods of another **registered** person. Same source.
- **ITC-04:** since 1 Oct 2021, **half-yearly** if the principal's preceding-year turnover exceeds **₹5 crore**, otherwise **annual**; due 25 days after the period (25 Oct, 25 Apr). https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/notfctn-35-central-tax-english-2021.pdf
- **Delivery challan:** Rule 55 allows a serially numbered challan, in triplicate, for transport of goods for job work; Rule 45(1) requires it from the principal, including goods sent straight to the job worker. [sec] https://icmai.in/upload/Taxation/Courses/Class12_CMA_RK_Khurana_Jobwork_Under_GST.pdf
- **Karigar registration:** he supplies a service, so registration is needed above **₹20 lakh** turnover (₹10 lakh in special category states; Kerala is ₹20 lakh). Returned goods count as the principal's supply, not his turnover. Section 22, same CBIC PDF. At ₹350 a gram, ₹20 lakh is about 5.7 kg of work a year.
- **Inter-state exception:** Notification 7/2017-Integrated Tax exempts job workers serving registered persons in other states from registration, but **excludes Chapter 71 jewellery** job workers, so a karigar serving an out-of-state jeweller must register (CA). [sec] https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gst-registration-exemption-job-workers-providing-interstate-supply-services-notified.html
- **Rate after GST 2.0:** Notification 15/2025-CT(Rate), effective 22 Sep 2025, entry 26 (SAC 9988): job work on Chapter 71 goods other than diamonds **2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST = 5%**; diamonds 1.5%; process on an unregistered owner's goods (not "job work") 18% (CA). Gazette text, third-party copy: https://bcasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/No.15-2025-Central-Tax-Rate.pdf Jewellery itself stays 3%. [sec] https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/gst-rate-for-jewellery-unchanged-at-3-pc-but-industry-sees-indirect-gains-125090401225_1.html
- **E-way bill, national:** inter-state job work normally needs one irrespective of value (https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/electronic-way-bill-050819.pdf), but Chapter 71 metals and jewellery are exempt under the Annexure to Rule 138(14), confirmed by GSTN on 6 Feb 2025. [sec] https://www.taxscan.in/gstn-withdraws-e-way-bill-generation-for-chapter-71-goods-ewb-mandate-continues-in-kerala-for-intra-state-movement/488167 Rule 138F (2023) lets a state require Part A for intra-state gold movement above a value it sets (floor ₹2 lakh). [sec] https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/intra-state-e-way-bill-rules-gst-gold-precious-stones.html
- **Kerala:** Notification 10/2024-State Tax, in force **20 Jan 2025**. https://keralataxes.gov.in/2017/10/13/gst-notifications/ Trade Circular 1/2025: consignments **₹10 lakh and above** (tax-inclusive for supplies), Part A only. **Job work and hallmarking trips** need an e-way bill at the start, with a delivery challan. **Every holder must record quantity held, quantity entrusted for job work or hallmarking, and balance in hand**, and show it on demand. [sec] https://www.jurishour.in/gst/kerala-gst-dept-issues-instructions-on-implementation-of-e-way-bill-for-movement-of-gold-and-precious-stones/

## 2. TDS

- **Contractor TDS:** section 194C until 31 Mar 2026; from 1 Apr 2026, **section 393(1), Table S.No. 6(i), Income-tax Act 2025**. **1% individual or HUF, 2% others**; threshold ₹30,000 per payment or ₹1,00,000 a year. https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/web/guest/w/tds-rates
- **Job work counts as "work"** when made from material bought from the customer; if the karigar uses his own gold it is a sale. TDS is on the **invoice value excluding material when material is shown separately**, otherwise the whole invoice. https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-194c-46 GST shown separately is excluded (CBDT Circular 23/2017). Confirm the 2025 Act keeps the material rule (CA).
- **194Q:** a buyer with turnover above ₹10 crore deducts **0.1%** on purchases above **₹50 lakh** a year from one seller. Relevant when a karigar sells finished ornaments on a sale bill. https://incometaxindia.gov.in/tutorials/71.tds-on-purchase-of-goods.pdf Now inside section 393; whether the ₹10 crore test survived is disputed (CA). [sec] https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/05/tds-on-purchase-of-goods-section-3931-section-194q/
- **Payment-delay rule:** money owed to a Udyam micro or small enterprise and unpaid after 15 days (45 with a written agreement) is deductible only when paid. Section 43B(h), now **37(2)(g)**. [sec] https://taxguru.in/income-tax/finance-ministry-clarifies-section-43bh-45-day-msme-payment-rule.html

## 3. PM Vishwakarma

From https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/ unless marked.
- **Goldsmith (Sunar) is covered**, one of 18 trades.
- **Eligibility:** 18+, hands-and-tools artisan in a listed family trade, self-employed, unorganised; no PMEGP, PM SVANidhi or MUDRA loan in 5 years; one per family; no government employees or their families.
- **Loans:** collateral-free **₹1 lakh (18 months), then ₹2 lakh (30 months)** at **5%** to the borrower; the ministry covers subvention up to 8% and the government pays the guarantee fee.
- **Toolkit** ₹15,000 as e-RUPI vouchers on BHIM, goldsmiths named. https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/press-releases/2024/BHIM-Press-Release-BHIM-to-Empower-Artisans-under-PM-Vishwakarma-Scheme-through-e-RUPI-Vouchers.pdf **Training** 5 to 7 days basic, 15 days advanced, ₹500 a day. **Digital incentive** ₹1 per transaction, up to 100 a month.
- **Dashboard, read 15 Nov 2026:** 30,00,000 registered; 24,43,704 trained; 18,92,624 toolkits; **6,39,800 loans for ₹5,498 crore**.
- **Goldsmith-only enrolment is not published** anywhere I found. Ask the district MSME office or file an RTI.

## 4. Other credit

- **MUDRA:** Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishore to ₹5 lakh; Tarun to ₹10 lakh; **Tarun Plus ₹10 to 20 lakh** for repaid Tarun borrowers. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2024/oct/doc20241029426401.pdf A MUDRA loan blocks PM Vishwakarma for 5 years.
- **Gold metal loans:** RBI/2025-26/124, **effective 1 Apr 2026**: jewellers who do not manufacture may borrow GML **only to outsource manufacture on job basis to firms, artisans or goldsmiths**; **ceiling 270 days** (non-exporters); repaid in INR. https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13194&Mode=0
- **Cooperative banks:** Kerala Bank (the state cooperative bank) offers gold loans and MSME loans such as KB Smart; no goldsmith product found. https://kerala.bank.in/p_msme_007.php Sahamati and NABARD ran workshops to bring all state cooperative banks onto Account Aggregator. [sec] https://sahamati.org.in/events/workshop-with-nabard-for-rrbs-and-state-cooperative-banks-on-the-process-to-join-aa/
- **TReDS:** companies above ₹250 crore turnover had to join invoice-discounting platforms by 31 Mar 2025, so a Udyam karigar unit can discount bills raised on big jewellers. [sec] https://taxmann.com/post/blog/companies-with-turnover-above-rs-250-crore-must-get-onboarded-on-trade-receivables-discounting-platforms
- **Fintech and NBFCs:** I found **no lender underwriting karigars on job-work records**. Closest: Bold Finance, which turns jewellery shops into gold-loan branches for a partner bank. [sec] https://inc42.com/buzz/exclusive-bold-finance-bags-seed-fund-from-kae-capital-others-to-3x-its-gold-loan-partners Indiagold and Ruptok do consumer gold loans. This is a search result, not proof none exist.

## 5. Consent-based data and payment rails

- **Account Aggregator:** RBI's NBFC-AA Directions 2025 (28 Nov 2025) replaced the 2016 Master Direction. Any entity regulated by a financial regulator can be a data user, so cooperative banks qualify. "Financial information" is a **fixed list** (deposits, securities, insurance and others, plus **GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B via GSTN**) that RBI may extend. [sec] https://taxguru.in/?p=1015044 GST data is live on AA. [sec] https://sahamati.org.in/rebit-publishes-gstn-data-schema-for-the-account-aggregator-framework
- **So a job-work ledger cannot travel on AA today.** A registered karigar's GSTR-1 (his labour bills) can.
- **DEPA:** NITI Aayog's 2020 draft of data-blind consent managers, AA being the finance version. https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2020-09/DEPA-Executive-Summary.pdf
- **DPDP Rules 2025:** notified 13 Nov 2025; **consent manager registration open from 13 Nov 2026**; most duties from 13 May 2027. [sec] https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/ The record holds the jeweller's data too, so take consent from both sides.
- **ULI (RBI's Unified Lending Interface):** consented flow of financial and **non-financial** data (land records, satellite data) to lenders; **64 lenders, 136+ data services** by Dec 2025. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2260497
- **How a cooperative bank uses the record:** today, the karigar shares a signed, verifiable record under DPDP consent; later, as a ULI data service, or on AA if RBI adds it.
- **Rails:** RBI lets bank pre-sanctioned credit lines fund UPI payments. https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/credit-line-on-upi e-RUPI vouchers are purpose-bound, ₹10,000 cap for private sponsors. https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/e-rupi/Product-Booklet.pdf UPI wages also earn the PM Vishwakarma digital incentive.

## 6. Labour and safety

- **All four labour codes in force from 21 Nov 2025.** https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2194018
- **Code on Social Security:** an unorganised worker is a home-based, self-employed or wage worker in the unorganised sector, meaning an individual-owned enterprise with **fewer than 10 workers** (s.2(85), 2(86)). Small-workshop karigars fit. [sec] https://taxclue.in/blog/section-2-social-security-code-2020-definitions-gig-worker-platform-worker-and-unorganised-worker/
- **e-Shram:** ages 16 to 59, not in EPFO, ESIC or government-funded NPS, not an income-tax payer. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/userfiles/file/e-ShramPortal3NCD.pdf
- **Aggregator risk:** "aggregators" pay 1 to 2% of turnover (capped at 5% of payouts) into a social security fund. If the product ever matches karigars to jewellers for a fee, check this (CA). https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2206144
- **OSH Code:** inter-state migrant rules apply to establishments with 10 or more such workers. [sec] https://ksandk.com/labour-employment/inter-state-migrant-workers-osh-code-2020/
- **Kerala:** guest worker registration on the **Athidhi portal** is mandatory since 2023; its ID unlocks benefits including Aawaz insurance. [sec] https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2024/09/04/migrant-workers-registration-mandatory-in-kerala-govt-to-launch-unified-portal-mobile-app.html The **Kerala Jewellery Workers' Welfare Fund Act, 2009** covers workers and self-employed in jewellery. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/21033 In 2019 only about 19,500 of nearly 3 lakh jewellery workers had enrolled. [sec] https://onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2019/10/30/gold-jewellery-workers-welfare-board-government-intervention.html

- **NCAER for GJEPC (2020, from the 6th Economic Census and NSS 73rd round):** India 9,89,372 gems and jewellery units, **42,88,926 workers** (25,37,419 in manufacturing). **Kerala 33,635 units, 1,33,012 workers (35,468 in manufacturing). Thrissur 11,044 units, 54,394 workers.** Trade-body study. https://gjepc.org/pdf/Cluster-Mapping-Study-of-the-Gems-&-Jewellery-Sector-in-India.pdf
- Kerala's welfare board story cites "nearly three lakh" jewellery workers (2019, link above). A 2007 trade quote puts Bengal and Odisha workers in Thrissur's gold units at 20,000 or more. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_business_in_Thrissur
- **No official goldsmith-only count found.**

## The 8 facts to lead with (CA and cooperative banker)

1. **The law already makes the jeweller keep the gold account** (s.143(2)); gold not back in a year is taxed as if supplied the day it left. Our lot record is that account.
2. **Kerala's gold e-way bill circular demands a running record** of gold held, with the karigar or hallmarker, and balance in hand. The product produces it by default.
3. **5% or 18%:** job work on gold is 5% only when the jeweller is registered. Whose metal and who is registered must be captured.
4. **TDS falls on labour only** (1% or 2%) when the gold value is shown separately. TDS moved to section 393 on 1 Apr 2026, so old bill templates are stale.
5. **Udyam karigar units must be paid within 15 or 45 days** or the jeweller loses that year's deduction (s.37(2)(g)). A signed delivery date starts the clock.
6. **From 1 Apr 2026 banks can lend gold to jewellers for job work by goldsmiths**, up to 270 days. The bank's gold sits with karigars, so the banker needs a verified lot trail.
7. **PM Vishwakarma covers goldsmiths:** ₹1 lakh then ₹2 lakh, collateral-free at 5%; 6.4 lakh loans sanctioned. A clean delivery record is what the second-tranche lender wants.
8. **AA carries GST returns but not work records; ULI already carries non-financial data; DPDP consent managers register from 13 Nov 2026.** A consented karigar record has a legal path to a cooperative bank now.
