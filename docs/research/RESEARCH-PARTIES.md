# Every Party in Gold Job Work: Published Research

Compiled 15 Nov 2026. **Weak** marks blogs, vendor pages, unopened snippets or pre-2015 data. Backbone source: [IIMA 2023][iima] (Varkkey and Jha, fieldwork 2021 to 22, 123 karigars in seven hubs). Page numbers refer to it.

## 1. How metal is issued and settled

- Two models: **outwork** (client supplies gold) and **outright** (maker's own metal). Large firms outsource 60 to 70% of manufacturing, mostly handmade ([IIMA p.24][iima]).
- Fabricators often give jewellers **1 to 3 months' credit** and fund metal with 180-day bank Gold Metal Loans ([WGC 2022][wgc]).
- Specialities: Mumbai machine-made, Coimbatore casting, Chennai and Kolkata handmade, Jaipur kundan, Surat diamond; 10 centres make 75 to 80% of output ([WGC][wgc]).
- **In the informal sector, wastage is the wage.** Head karigars are allowed 2 to 3% on plain, 20 to 30% on studded, 4% on antique; they allow karigars 3 to 4% (plain) and 15 to 20% (studded). Karigars keep what they save, about "1.3 grams per 100 grams" ([IIMA p.34][iima]). Ankurhati park: "Karigars get paid in waste. 1.5% of 1 kg gold is their revenue" ([IIMA p.49][iima]).
- Semi-formal piece rates: chains ₹10 to 12/g, necklaces ₹23 to 30/g, bangles ₹5 to 10/g, targets 400 to 650 g a month ([IIMA p.30][iima]).
- Squaring up: a register per karigar records gold received, finished weight, dust collected and lost; the head karigar checks it after every job and pays **weekly or twice a month** ([IIMA p.38][iima]).
- Trust ladder: new karigars get **100 to 250 g**; a Kolkata karigar holds ₹4 to 5 lakh of gold at a time ([IIMA p.45][iima]).
- Hub notes: Coimbatore goldsmiths say they are "only allowed to take one or two per cent" ([Covai Post 2016][covai], weak) and bulk buyers keep cutting it ([DT Next 2020][dtn20]). Hyderabad goldsmiths "used to earn some profit from gold wastage", now only crafting charges ([Hans India 2021][hans21]). Rajkot shops moved customer labour from ₹100 to 250/g to 15 to 20% of gold value ([Business Standard 2011][bs11], old).
- Benchmark: DGFT export norms from Jan 2025 allow **2.25% wastage on handmade gold, 0.45% machine-made** ([Taxguru][dgft]). A Nagpur jeweller puts real loss at 0.5 to 2% ([Londe][nagpur], weak).
- **Not found:** karigar rates for Thrissur, Jaipur or Kolkata; temple-jewellery wastage; written wastage contracts; any source for "settai".

## 2. Karigar life

- **No official count exists** ([ThePrint 2017][print]). The sector employs about 4.65 million ([WGC][wgc]); about 6.5 lakh Bengali karigars nationally is an industry estimate ([Retail Jeweller][rj], weak, not opened).
- Mumbai: 1 to 1.5 lakh artisans until 2016 ([ThePrint][print]). In 2025 work per karigar fell from 500 g to 100 g a month, pay from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000 ([Art of Jewellery/TOI][aoj]).
- Thrissur: about 3,000 units, 40,000 artisans, 20,000+ from Bengal and Odisha ([Wikipedia citing The Hindu 2007][wiki], old). Over 80% of surveyed Kerala gold workers are from northern India, concentrated in larger units of 30 to 45 workers ([Sumeetha M., EPW 2019][christ]).
- Coimbatore: about 50,000 workers, down from 1 lakh ([DT Next 2020][dtn20]). Chennai: about 5,000 units; 210 bonded labourers rescued in 15 months; one worker paid ₹10,000 against a ₹21,500 minimum ([DT Next 2023][dtn23]). Hyderabad: about 1.5 lakh Old City Bengalis, 90% goldsmith families, earning ₹5,000 to 8,000 ([Hans India 2021][hans21]).
- Pay: informal karigars average ₹19,562 a month, remit about ₹5,000; head karigars about ₹45,000; no PF or insurance; emergencies covered by head karigar, peers or moneylenders ([IIMA p.34 to 35][iima]).
- **Delayed pay:** in Zaveri Bazaar workers draw weekly cash advances and get the balance only when they go home, once or twice a year ([ASK 2014][ask], old).
- Hours average 14, over 16 from October to December ([IIMA p.41][iima]). Apprentices ("chokra") start at about 16, unpaid for 2 to 5 years ([IIMA p.36][iima]).
- Workplace: head karigar's home or rented room, 5 to 6 men in 10x12 ft, living where they work, behind double iron doors; no women ([IIMA p.38, p.40][iima]).
- Most entrants studied to 7th standard at most; workshops run in Bengali ([IIMA p.41][iima]). **No survey of karigar smartphone use found.**

## 3. Disputes and losses

- **No official frequency data.** Free Press Journal alone reported **8 Mumbai-region cases of karigars vanishing with entrusted gold, Jan 2024 to Jan 2026, about 8.1 kg**: [550 g][f1], [1,497 g][f2], [1,536 g][f3], [2,689 g][f4], [200 g][f5], [256 g and 650 g][f6], [763 g][f7].
- Patterns: relationships of 5 and 7 years, gold handed over in several lots, partial returns, a **178 g leftover balance of "wire, powder, hooks, beads" from earlier jobs** ([f4]). None of the five reports I checked mentions a receipt or register.
- Elsewhere: Thrissur 2,700 g ([2006][thr06], old); Coimbatore 520 g ([2017][cbe17]); Chennai 700 g ([DT Next][chn]); Jaipur 600 g ([inkl][jpr], undated). In a theft the head karigar repays the client ([IIMA p.38][iima]).
- **Karigars wronged:** Rajkot, Oct 2023, two Bengal artisans beaten to death over 100 g of suspected silver theft ([Deccan Herald][rjk]). Thrissur, Sep 2023, an ex-employee owed ₹15 lakh by a chain maker staged a ₹1.8 crore robbery to recover dues ([Onmanorama][jpc]). Hyderabad Bengali workers cite "regular harassment" ([Hans India 2018][hans18]).

## 4. Customer pressure

- Peaks: Deepavali, Ramzan, Akshaya Tritiya and weddings, with 14+ hour days ([DT Next 2023][dtn23]).
- **Durga Puja exodus:** Zaveri Bazaar's Bengali karigars go home every year mid-season, so wedding pieces run late ([DNA 2011][dna], old but recurring).
- Turnarounds promised: mangalsutras in 10 to 15 days ([f2]); a necklace set in about a week ([f1]). Bhopal, Apr 2026: a gas shortage stretched 2 to 4 day jobs to 15 days at wedding peak ([FPJ][bpl]).
- The head karigar sets gold needs and timelines with karigars per design ([IIMA p.35][iima]).
- Liability: NCDRC (Saundarya Jewellers, 2022) ordered delivery or refund with interest plus ₹40,000 ([Dainik Bhaskar][bhaskar]). **Not found:** how late costs pass to karigars.

## 5. Refiners and scrap

- Karigars box desk dust; agencies collect floor and clothing dust, swapping mats yearly (sometimes paying ₹500 to 700) and clothes weekly ([IIMA p.38][iima]). Some work shirtless to save dust (p.33).
- Credit: under wastage deals the saved gold is the karigar's pay; Ankurhati sells dust to refiners under GST ([IIMA p.49][iima]).
- Drain-sludge sweepers (Nehariyas, Kadaiwallas) work the street layer ([Tribune 2011][trib], weak).
- 60 to 65% of recycling was unorganised in 2021; jewellers prefer local refiners for speed and cash ([WGC refining][wgcref]). Scrap arriving is often 70 to 85% pure ([WGC][wgc]).
- **Not found:** any Indian recovery rate for sweeps or polishing dust.

## 6. Hallmarking

- Job workers need **no BIS registration**, only proof of job-work status ([BIS FAQ 2, 7][bisj]). The first seller hallmarks; the jeweller or the AHC raises the request ([BIS FAQ 4, 22][bisj]).
- AHCs must deliver **within 48 hours**, first in first out; a piece short "even by one grade" is rejected, and the sender is responsible ([BIS AHC FAQ][bisa]).
- HUID is a 6-character laser mark, mandatory since June 2021 ([WGC][wgc]). Consumers get **double the purity shortfall** under BIS Rules 2018 ([Bhaskar][bhaskar]).

## 7. Existing software

- **Tally:** Material Out, "Stock With Job Worker", variance reports; owner only, no touch ([Tally][tally]). **Marg:** issue, receive, wastage, labour bill ([Codingclave][marg], weak). **Online Munim:** issue and receive, business-run supplier panel ([Techjockey][munim]).
- **SwarnApp:** offline, karigar jobs and payments ([Techjockey][swarn]); its **Jewelsteps** app lets workers post status and photos, with no karigar balance or receipt confirmation ([Jewelsteps][jsteps]). **JewelAcc** tracks fine or net weight ([App Store][jacc]); **Joolet** controls loss "by percentage or touch" ([Joolet][joolet]).
- **Goldsetu:** retailer storefront, no karigar feature ([YourStory][gset]). **Jwelly:** admin reports app ([App Store][jwelly], weak). **Omunim:** nothing karigar-specific found.
- Karigar-facing apps exist only per jeweller, such as Mehta Gold Karigar for order status ([App Store][mehta]). **None found where the karigar sees his own balance, confirms weights, or keeps a portable record.**

## 8. Insider facts a generic app gets wrong

1. "Reduce wastage" cuts the karigar's pay ([IIMA][iima]).
2. Often three parties: jeweller, head karigar ("babu"), karigar ([IIMA p.44][iima]).
3. Balances run across jobs and years ([f4], [f6]).
4. Absconders "often change mobile numbers" ([FPJ][f1]).
5. The karigar's own wage is held by someone else ([ASK][ask]).
6. Touch must be measured: AHCs reject on one grade ([BIS][bisa]).
7. GST on making is 18% for an individual supplying bullion, 5% for a registered dealer ([WGC][wgc]); confirm with a CA.
8. Durga Puja sets real delivery dates in Bengali-staffed hubs ([DNA][dna]).

## Ten insights for a product fair to both sides

1. Record the **wastage allowance as an agreed term per item**; show savings as the karigar's earnings.
2. Model the **head karigar** as a party who receives and re-issues metal.
3. Keep one **running fine-gold balance per relationship**, leftovers and partial returns included, visible to both.
4. **Both sides confirm each handover** with weight, touch and photo.
5. Let a **verified history raise issue limits** beyond the 100 to 250 g starting lot.
6. Give the karigar **his own wage and advance ledger**.
7. Anchor identity in more than a phone number.
8. Credit **dust and sweep recoveries** to whoever owns them under the deal.
9. Build **festival and home-trip calendars** into delivery promises.
10. Use the **DGFT 2.25% and 0.45% norms** as a neutral reference when no wastage term was written.
