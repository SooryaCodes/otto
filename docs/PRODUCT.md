# The product, for every party (v3, 15 Nov 2026)


## The one idea
A **shared gold ledger between everyone who handles the metal**, run by an agent, that turns each karigar's work into a record both sides sign and a bank can trust.

Three layers:
1. **The handover** (built): photo of the scale, AI reads it, code checks it, the other side confirms, locked. The atomic trust event.
2. **The relationship** (new): every pair of parties has one account with agreed terms (wastage, labour, settlement, payment days, who hallmarks). An agent runs every open order against those terms: deadlines, wastage, scrap, settlement, payment, papers.
3. **The record** (new): the karigar's work passport. Gold handled, on time, wastage against agreed, disputes, payments received. Shared only with the karigar's consent, with a bank, a cooperative or a new jeweller.

## Two ways a ledger gets started
- **Jeweller-led:** a shop issues gold to many karigars. The shop's staff use the app; karigars confirm on WhatsApp.
- **Workshop-led:** a unit like ours works for big shops that will not use any app. The shop's signed copy and its scale photo stand in for its confirmation, as today.
Both write the same kind of entry. Whichever side is not on the app confirms by WhatsApp or by a signed paper photographed. Nothing depends on both sides installing anything.

## Fairness rules (fairness matters most to the karigar, who has less power)
1. No entry can be edited by one side. Corrections are new entries both confirm.
2. Every karigar has full access to his own account, in his language, at any time.
3. Settlements are drafted, never posted: both sides approve, or the karigar questions a line.
4. Payment days are part of the terms. Late payment shows on both sides, and the agent reminds the jeweller, not only the karigar.
5. The work passport belongs to the karigar. Nobody sees it without his consent, and consent expires.
6. Customers never see the karigar's name or rates.

## What the agent does (agentic AI bar)
- **Plans:** works back from the customer's date to each karigar's due date, with a buffer for hallmarking.
- **Acts:** sends WhatsApp confirmations and reminders, drafts challans, bills, settlements and the job-work register, logs scrap to the refiner.
- **Remembers:** each relationship's terms, each karigar's usual wastage per item, each shop's scale gap.
- **Recovers:** a misread photo, a missing confirmation, a late piece: it asks again, escalates, or replans.
- **Asks a person:** before any money moves, any settlement is sent, any data is shared.

## Every size and every model (15 Nov 2026)
**Sizes:** solo karigar (rural units average 1.9 workers); head karigar's room (5 to 6 karigars); small job-work unit (5 to 15); mid unit like ours (15 to 20, own building, tools, often own gold); large manufacturer (30 to 45+ per unit in Thrissur, hundreds in factories; Gujarat averages 12.8 per unit); small family jeweller (one shop); regional chain (in-house karigars, units like ours and outside karigars at once); national chain (own factories plus vendors and artisans); wholesalers, bullion dealers and banks that supply or lend metal.

**Arrangements, chosen per relationship:** whose metal (giver's, maker's own, mixed, bank's on loan); labour basis (per gram, piece, pair, lump sum, wastage as the wage, touch-based); settlement timing (per job, weekly, twice a month, monthly, when the worker goes home); who hallmarks; how workers are employed (salaried, daily, piece rate, wastage-paid, unpaid apprentice); where the work happens (bench, home, karigar park on the shop's land); how many hands (2, 3 or 4); payment form (bank with TDS, cash, gold, advances); tax status (registered or not, same or other state); kind of order (stock, custom or bridal with a date, repair).

**Why one product fits all:** every model is a chain of handover pairs. Each pair has terms set once and a way of signing (bench, WhatsApp, or a signed paper and scale photo). The handover never changes; only settings do.

**Three ways in, nothing else:**
- **The app** (phone or tablet; the same app on a shop computer): anyone who gives out gold (jewellers, units, head karigars). Issue and receive at the scale, see every relationship, approve papers and settlements.
- **WhatsApp:** people who receive gold or need updates (karigars, workers away from the bench, delivery staff, customers).
- **A link** (any phone browser, no install, no password): people who need to see or do one thing (karigar's own account, refiner's batch, bank's consented record, CA's register, café's assisted set-up).
- **Bench mode:** in-house workers confirm at a shared device at the scale with their photo and a PIN, or by voice.
- **Platforms:** the owner app is Android first, since most shop staff, units and karigars use Android. Everyone else uses WhatsApp or a link.

## How the hard cases are handled (designed, not built yet)
- **A jeweller who never signs.** The karigar logs what he received himself: a photo of the scale, a photo of the jeweller's slip, and the time. Otto AI sends it to the jeweller on WhatsApp. If nobody answers, the entry stays in his record as **one-sided**, with both photos. Nobody can delete it, and it becomes two-sided the day the jeweller confirms. Screen: KarigarLog.
- **When the numbers differ.** First the usual gap between the two scales is checked (for example 0.20 to 0.30 g). A bigger difference is re-weighed together on a third scale both sides named at set-up, such as a hallmarking centre. If they are still apart, a neutral person both named at set-up decides, such as the local gold workers' association. Both sign the result; every weighing stays in the record. Screen: DisputeResolve.
- **Wastage evidence.** With every return the karigar weighs back scrap (filings, dust, cut-offs) next to the piece, so issued = piece + scrap + loss, to the milligram. Scrap goes to the refiner in batches; the fine gold recovered is credited back to each karigar by his share of the batch. Where no wastage term was written, the DGFT norms (2.25% handmade, 0.45% machine-made) are shown as a neutral reference. Screen: ScrapReturn.
- **A karigar working for several jewellers.** His account spans all of them. With his consent, a jeweller's Planner sees only that he is busy on certain dates, never for whom.
- **Helpers.** A karigar can add his helpers; their work is counted under him, and each helper can see his own lines by link.
- **Customers.** Only if they opt in. The jeweller approves every customer message, and the customer sees status and date only, never the karigar's name or rates.
- **Head karigars.** Two linked settlements: jeweller to head karigar, and head karigar to each of his karigars.
- **Cash, gold and part payments.** Confirmed by both sides like a weighing, so a cash payment leaves the same trail as a bank transfer.
- **Refiners.** Each batch is logged with weight and photo; the assay certificate is read from a photo; the recovered fine gold is credited and split.
- **Tamper evidence.** Every entry carries a hash of the entry before it, so any change breaks the chain. Exports for a CA, a bank or a court carry a certificate for the electronic record (to be confirmed with a lawyer).
- **Consent and data.** Only what the work needs: names, phone numbers, weights, photos and amounts, never Aadhaar. Every share has its own consent text and an end date. Records are kept as long as tax law requires.
- **Platforms.** The owner app is Android first. Everyone else uses WhatsApp or a link.
